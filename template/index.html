MY APPROACH

I care about what the numbers mean for people.

I am a Data Analyst with a Computer Science background and an interest in helping businesses understand their data, improve reporting, and make more informed decisions.

My approach starts with understanding the question a business needs to answer. From there, I work to organise the data, explore meaningful patterns, and communicate the findings in clear, practical language.

I work with tools such as Excel, SQL, and Power BI. My interests include sales performance, customer behaviour, SaaS analytics, and business reporting.

I believe good analysis should do more than present numbers. It should help people understand what those numbers mean, recognise limitations, and decide what to investigate next.
